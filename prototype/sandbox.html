Cold vs warm launch

Launches the FEG-provided game package, served unmodified from a separate origin. Hovering or keyboard-focusing a tile expresses intent; the governor decides whether to spend bytes; warming is done by the prototype's own warmer before the iframe is created.

SANDBOX No provider backend. The game reaches engine start, not playable — its preloader never clears because api.spiniq.io is unreachable.

Cold vs warm launch

Launches the FEG-provided game package, served unmodified from a separate origin. Intent expressed by pointer, keyboard, or touch climbs a speculation ladder: a transport hint costs nothing, a short dwell buys the blocking asset profile, and a sustained dwell buys a pre-initialised engine. The governor decides how far up the ladder this device is allowed to go.

SANDBOX No provider backend. The game reaches engine start, not playable — its preloader never clears because api.spiniq.io is unreachable.
